ChewyHub Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

ChewyHub is a private app for friends and family of Nick Chu to watch and listen to the movies, TV shows and music on Nick's own media server ("ChewyNeuraLink"), which runs the free Jellyfin software. ChewyHub is independent and is not made by or affiliated with the Jellyfin project or Tailscale.

The short version

  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics. ChewyHub doesn't sell or share your data.
  • Your account details are stored on Nick's server, and only Nick can see them.
  • To connect you privately, ChewyHub uses Tailscale, which learns some technical details about your phone (see below).

What stays on your phone

A sign-in key from the server (never your password), the private-connection settings for your phone, your app settings, listening statistics, recent searches and anything you download. Deleting the app removes all of this.

What's stored on Nick's server

  • Your account: username and password (the password is stored by Jellyfin in scrambled, hashed form), favorites, playlists, what you've watched or listened to and where you stopped, as Jellyfin always keeps.
  • Your sign-up details: first and last name, email address and (optionally) phone number. They're used only so Nick can approve your account and so you can reset your password by email.
  • Your devices: the name each of your phones has on the private network (for example chewyhub-sam-1a2b3c), so they can be removed when you delete your account.
  • Sharing: when you share a playlist, other people on the server can see your username, and the people you share with can see and play that playlist.

Tailscale (the private connection)

When you sign in, ChewyHub joins Nick's private Tailscale network so it can reach the server without putting it on the open internet. Tailscale, Inc. runs the service that introduces your phone to the server. To do that it receives:

  • the device name above (which includes your username), your phone's operating system and Tailscale version,
  • your phone's IP addresses and an encryption key for the connection.

Your movies, music and other traffic travel encrypted end to end between your phone and the server; Tailscale can't read them, even when it relays them. ChewyHub switches off Tailscale's optional diagnostic logs. Only ChewyHub's own traffic uses this connection, not the rest of your phone. Tailscale's privacy policy: tailscale.com/privacy-policy

Sign-up and password reset happen before you're on the private network, so they go through Nick's public sign-up address (also provided by Tailscale), encrypted with HTTPS.

Camera and photos

ChewyHub uses your camera or photo library only if you choose a picture as a playlist cover.

Your choices

  • Edit your name, email and phone in Settings → Account → Your profile.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Account → Delete my account. This deletes your Jellyfin account, your sign-up details and your phones' entries on the private network.
  • Signing out also takes your phone off the private network.

Children

ChewyHub isn't directed at children under 13, and accounts are only for people Nick approves.
